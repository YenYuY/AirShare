# AirShare App Store Copy (English)

## Name

AirShare

## Subtitle

Local Photo & Video Transfer

## Promotional Text

Move photos and videos from your iPhone to a computer over the same Wi-Fi—without an account, cable, or cloud upload.

## Description

AirShare lets you transfer photos and videos directly from your iPhone to a computer over your local Wi-Fi network.

There is no account to create, no desktop software to install, and no need to upload your media to the cloud first. Start AirShare, enter the address shown in the app in your computer's browser, then browse and download the original files you need.

Key features:

• Fast transfers on the same Wi-Fi network
• Opens directly in your computer's web browser
• Supports photos and videos
• Downloads original files
• Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese interfaces
• No account required
• No third-party cloud storage step

How it works:

1. Allow AirShare to access your photo library
2. Connect your iPhone and computer to the same Wi-Fi
3. Start sharing in AirShare
4. Enter the displayed address in your computer's browser
5. Choose and download your photos or videos

Keep AirShare open and your iPhone awake while transferring files.
