## Anti-patterns verdict

The page does not read as generic AI output. It has a committed local-network-appliance visual direction, real product imagery, restrained containment, and a clear platform story. The deterministic detector reported zero findings in `index.html`.

## What works

- The paired iOS and Android presentation makes platform support immediately visible.
- The navy/aqua palette communicates a trustworthy local connection without relying on security clichés.
- The three-step flow, privacy statement, and platform comparison answer the main pre-download questions.

## Issues found and resolved

- **[P1] Chinese headings could break between semantic phrases.** Replaced browser-dependent character wrapping with locale-authored semantic lines and strict Chinese line-breaking rules.
- **[P1] Opening copy was too technical.** Rewrote “temporary local sharing service” and “middleman” language into a concrete phone → Wi-Fi → browser workflow.
- **[P2] Keyboard focus was under-specified.** Added high-contrast `:focus-visible` treatment and improved language control labeling.
- **[P2] Image and device labels were not localized.** Added localized accessible names and iOS screenshot alt text.

## Persona checks

- **First-time visitor:** Can now understand the transfer in one read without translating networking jargon.
- **Privacy-conscious user:** Sees that files remain on the local network and that AirShare has no receiving cloud backend.
- **Narrow-screen mobile visitor:** Chinese display copy now breaks at authored phrase boundaries rather than between arbitrary characters.

## Remaining minor observation

The Android section currently leads to product information rather than a Google Play listing. Add a direct store badge only when the public listing URL is available.
