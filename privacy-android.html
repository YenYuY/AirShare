Overview

AirShare lets an Android device share photos and videos directly with a browser on the same local network. AirShare does not require an account and does not use a developer-operated backend, cloud storage, advertising, analytics, or remote crash reporting.

The developer cannot view, receive, or recover the photos, videos, or browsing activity involved in an AirShare transfer.

Information AirShare accesses

Photos and videos

With your permission, AirShare reads the photos and videos that Android makes available to the app. This access is used to display your media library, create local thumbnails, and make media available during a sharing session.

While sharing is active, a browser using the sharing link receives gallery metadata and thumbnails as it browses. An original photo or video is sent only when the browser requests it. These transfers go directly to the receiving device; AirShare does not upload the media library to the developer or to cloud storage.

Local network information

AirShare uses local network addresses and connection status to run a temporary sharing service and show you how to connect from another device. This information is processed on your devices and is not sent to the developer.

Sharing-session information

AirShare exposes a temporary local HTTP address while sharing is active. The address is used only between the Android device and browsers on the local network, and the local server closes when sharing ends.

App preferences and operational state

AirShare may store non-personal preferences on your Android device. Temporary transfer status and errors may be held locally for the active session. AirShare does not transmit this information to the developer.

How information is used

AirShare uses the information described above only to:

  • show the media library that Android has authorized the app to access;
  • create and operate a local sharing session;
  • serve the local gallery while sharing is active;
  • display thumbnails and deliver a requested original photo or video; and
  • show connection, progress, and error status on your devices.

AirShare does not use your information for advertising, analytics, profiling, sale, or cross-app tracking.

Transfers and disclosure

Transfers are initiated by the user and occur directly between the Android device and the browser over HTTP on the local network. There is no developer-operated relay or storage service. Local HTTP traffic is not encrypted and may be observable by other parties on an untrusted or compromised network.

Anyone who has the active link and access to the same local network may be able to use the sharing session. Only start AirShare on a network you trust, share the link only with the intended recipient, and stop sharing when you are finished.

The copy downloaded by the recipient is controlled by that recipient and by the browser or operating system they use. AirShare cannot delete a copy after it has been downloaded to another device.

Retention and deletion

AirShare has no developer-operated account or backend database and therefore retains no account data, media, or transfer history on the developer's systems.

The local sharing server closes when sharing stops. Local app preferences remain on the Android device until you clear AirShare's app data or uninstall the app. You can delete downloaded media from the receiving device using that device's normal file-management controls.

Because AirShare does not create user accounts, there is no AirShare account to delete.

Permissions and your choices

AirShare requests photo and video access because browsing and sharing the device's media library is its core function. Depending on the Android version, you may grant full access, grant access to selected media, deny access, or later change the permission in Android Settings.

On Android 17 and later, local-network access is separately requested when you start sharing. You can grant or revoke it in Android Settings.

If you deny or limit access, AirShare will show only the media Android allows it to read, and some full-library browsing features may be unavailable. You can stop network access at any time by ending the sharing session.

Policy website hosting

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you choose to open it, your browser connects to GitHub, which may process ordinary website request information under its own privacy statement. AirShare does not send your photo library or local transfer content to GitHub.

Security

AirShare uses a temporary local sharing service without an access token, and HTTP traffic is not encrypted. Anyone on the same network who knows the address may be able to access the shared media while sharing is active. Use AirShare only on a trusted private network and end the session promptly after use.

Children's privacy

AirShare is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other user on developer-operated systems.
