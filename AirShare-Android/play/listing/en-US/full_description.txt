Move photos and videos from your Android phone to any computer on the same Wi-Fi network.

AirShare turns your phone into a temporary, encrypted local sharing service. Start sharing, scan the QR code or open the displayed address in a browser, then browse thumbnails and download original photos or videos.

Why AirShare

• No account or sign-in
• No cloud upload
• No desktop app to install
• Encrypted HTTPS transfer on your local network
• Temporary session token
• Clear controls to start and stop sharing
• Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese

AirShare has no analytics and no developer-operated backend. Your media travels directly between your phone and the authorized browser on your local network.

The secure certificate is created temporarily on your phone, so your browser may show a local certificate warning. Compare the SHA-256 fingerprint shown by AirShare before continuing.

Photo and video access is used to display and share the media library while you run a sharing session. Only people who have the active session link and access to the same local network can connect. Use AirShare on a network you trust and stop sharing when you are finished.
